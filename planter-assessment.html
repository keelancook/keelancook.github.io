Before You Begin

Built for a pastor, elder, or trained assessor with standing to ask personal questions, who has already reviewed the candidate's self-evaluations and a preaching sample.

Complete this after the interview to capture what you actually observed. It produces a growth plan and an endorsement decision, so detail matters more than speed.

Before You Start This Evaluation
  • You've reviewed the candidate's self-evaluations on character, calling, and competency.
  • You've reviewed a sample of his preaching or teaching.
  • You've conducted a thorough, unhurried interview with the candidate, and his spouse if married.
