The Coffee Cup Assessment

A first conversation with a potential church planter shouldn't feel like an interview. It should feel like coffee. This guide helps you listen well while you talk.

Nothing here is stored on this website. Everything stays in your browser and disappears when you close the page. Save it as a PDF before you go.

What This Is (and Isn't)

This is an informal tool. It has no score to pass or fail and produces no endorsement. Formal assessment comes later, with trained assessors and structured interviews.

This is lighter: a way to pay attention during an early, informal conversation, usually over coffee, with someone curious about church planting.

Optional. Appears at the top of your saved report.
How to Use This

Have the conversation naturally, then fill this out afterward based on what you heard.

Twelve prompts across three areas: character, calling, competency. Mark what you noticed, add notes at the end of each.
