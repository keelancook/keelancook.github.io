Short-Term Trip Security Checklist

A conversation guide for trip leaders and sending church leaders to work through with the field contact before finalizing plans for a short-term mission trip.

Nothing here is stored on this website. Everything you type stays in your browser and disappears when you close the page. Print or save it as a PDF before you go.

Handle these answers carefully. Once filled out, this document may contain sensitive information about people and places. Don't send it over unencrypted channels unless your field contact has told you it's safe to do so. Use Signal or another private method to communicate, and always defer to your field contact's preferred way of communicating.

01
Section One
Security Context

What is the current security level for foreigners in this country or region, particularly those associated with religious work?

Are there specific government agencies, monitoring programs, or known surveillance practices we should be aware of?

Have previous teams had phones searched, inspected, detained, or confiscated at the border or in-country?

Is the security situation currently stable, or has it changed recently in ways that affect how we should prepare?

Does our team need an STS (short truthful statement) for this trip? If so, what should we use?

These questions determine what you need below. If this is a low-security or open trip, the remaining sections may not be necessary. But if your field contact has flagged security concerns, walk through the rest of this checklist to build a full security profile for the trip.

02
Section Two
Before You Go

What app or apps do you want our team using to communicate with you and with each other in-country (Signal, WhatsApp, etc.), and is there a VPN you'd recommend we install before we arrive?

Are there concerns about cloud sync, location services, or other phone settings we should address before we arrive?

What can we tell supporters they can and cannot say on our behalf during the trip? Can we name the country, region, or city in prayer requests?

Should all team communication back home go through a private channel, or are general public updates acceptable?

What language, terms, or code words should we use, and avoid, across any communication, whether spoken, public, private, or in digital messages and group chats?

03
Section Three
During the Trip & Immigration

Are there any specific suggestions concerning immigration, especially concerning our phones or other tech?

Are there apps you want removed or hidden from home screens before arriving at the border?

What is your policy for whether team members can post anything on social media during the trip?

Can team members photograph local believers? Under what conditions, and with what level of consent required?

Can team members photograph you or other field workers? What is your preference?

Are there specific locations, buildings, neighborhoods, or gathering places that should never be photographed?

How do you want us to handle prayer and trip updates to supporters while we're in-country? Is a private Signal group appropriate, or do you prefer no real-time communication?

04
Section Four
After You Return

What is your protocol for photos after the team returns home? Is there a clearance process before anything is shared publicly?

Is there a waiting period after the trip before any photos or stories can be shared publicly?

How should we handle the church presentation and post-trip sharing? Would you want to review before we present publicly?

Is there anything from this trip that should remain permanently private, even after we return home?
