Peoples Next Door · Diaspora Engagement

Is Your Church Ready for the Peoples Next Door?

The nations are no longer only overseas. Millions of people from the least-reached people groups in the world now live in American cities, many of them within driving distance of evangelical churches that have never once thought about engaging them.

How This Works

This assessment has 20 questions across four tiers. Answer each one honestly based on your church's current reality. There are no right answers to perform.

At the end, you'll get a scored breakdown by tier with interpretation and specific next steps for where you are.
